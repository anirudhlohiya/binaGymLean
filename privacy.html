Who handles it for us

Your details are stored in Google Sheets (Google) and processed by our own automation server. Payments go through Razorpay. Emails are sent with Gmail. The page fonts load from Google Fonts. We do not sell or rent your details to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop marketing messages any time: reply STOP on WhatsApp, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or write to us.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing gyaansharma22@gmail.com.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. It does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.

Children

This website and our plans are for adults. If you are under 18, please do not send us your details.

Complaints

For any concern about your data, write to gyaansharma22@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Changes
