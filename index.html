Questions

Do I need any equipment?

No. A mat or a carpet, a timer on your phone and water. That's it.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Every move has an easier version, like wall push-ups and chair squats. Start there.

I live in a flat. Can I do this without jumping?

Yes. Use step jacks instead of jumping jacks and march instead of high knees.

Is the food veg?

The ebook has a full veg week and a full non-veg week. Use whichever fits you.

How do I get the ebook after paying?

You go straight to a download page, and we email you the link too. It's a PDF that opens on any phone.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's a simple home plan for healthy adults. If you have a health problem, an injury, or you are pregnant, ask your doctor first.
